## Notes

- **Frame-independent metric.** Positions are not compared across frames; only family composition and internal feature–feature distances are, exactly as in the original evaluation.
- **The directional acceptor is the one soft spot** — where a family is a directional H-bond acceptor its geometry Δ can inflate, consistent with the documented ring-flip sensitivity; the pose-invariant cation/aromatic/hydrophobe anchors are recovered cleanly.
- **Reproducibility.** Deterministic given the same docked inputs and config; re-run with `pixi run python scripts/reconstruct_findings.py`.
